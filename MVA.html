MVA Notification Cycle

Motor Vehicle Accident Protocol & Procedures

📋 Purpose

Ensure timely, accurate communication to all management levels when a company unit is involved in a motor vehicle accident and provide prompt care and continuity of service to any patients on board.

Notification Flow

INCIDENT OCCURS
↓
Dispatcher Confirms Details & Notifies SSC
↓
Dispatch SSC Sends Management Page
↓
Dispatch SSC Notifies Management Chain

Step 1: Dispatcher Immediate Actions

Upon Notification of an MVA:

  • ✓ Confirm unit number
  • ✓ Confirm location
  • ✓ Confirm nature of incident
  • ✓ Confirm injuries (if any)
  • ✓ Confirm patient status (loaded/unloaded)
  • ✓ Document time in incident log

If Unit is Patient-Loaded:

  • ✓ Dispatch backup unit to retrieve patient
  • ✓ Dispatch assessment unit for all parties
  • ✓ Notify on-shift Dispatch SSC

Note: If no SSC on duty, dispatcher completes all remaining tasks

Step 2: Management Page

Send immediately following initial notification

Recipients:

• Entire Management Page

• On-Shift Ground Supervisor

• Area Manager

• Jerry Plyler (Director of Risk Management)

Include in Page:

• Unit number involved

• Location of MVA

• Patient status (loaded/unloaded)

• Any injuries or scene hazards

Document: Record time and method in incident log

Step 3: SSC Notification Chain

Upon being informed by dispatcher, notify in order:

  1. Communications Manager
  2. Director of Communications
  3. Director of Risk Management
  4. Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Documentation: Record all notifications in MVA Notification Log including date/time, method, and person notified

  • All times must be recorded in Central Time
  • MVA Notification Log must be submitted to Communications Manager within 24 hours
  • Prompt documentation ensures compliance and accurate incident tracking
